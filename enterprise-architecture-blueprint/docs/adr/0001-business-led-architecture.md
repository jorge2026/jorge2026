# ADR 0001: Business-led, capability-based architecture

- **Status:** Accepted for this illustrative case study
- **Date:** 2026-11-21

## Context

Technology initiatives need traceability to business outcomes. A purely technology-led inventory does not explain why capabilities should change or how investments create value.

## Decision

Use business drivers, stakeholder concerns, capability maps, and value streams as the entry point. Trace these through process, application, data, and technology architecture to transition work packages and measures.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Clearer traceability from strategy to implementation.
- Better communication between business and technology stakeholders.
- More consistent prioritization and architecture governance.
- A basis for measuring business and technology outcomes.

### Trade-offs

- Requires stakeholder participation and business ownership.
- Capability and value-stream models need ongoing maintenance.
- Benefits cannot be claimed without baseline data and measurement.

## Follow-up

Validate capability definitions, owners, value streams, and KPIs with real stakeholders before adapting this reference model to an organization.
